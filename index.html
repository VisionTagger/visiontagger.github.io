Overview

VisionTagger generates rich, structured metadata for your photos entirely on-device. Privacy-first, it processes images locally on Apple Silicon (M1 or later) with macOS Tahoe or newer. No data leaves your Mac.

Key Benefits

Custom Metadata Schema
Define titles, descriptions, keywords, content & style, safety checks, and custom fields with tailored prompts. Supports Boolean, Text, or List types to match your workflow.

Flexible Outputs
Create XMP sidecars, embed via ExifTool, export JSON/TXT, write to Photos Library, or apply Finder tags. Handles JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, WebP from folders or Photos.

On-Device Vision Models
Download preconfigured models in-app or use your own GGUF vision model + projector. Fast, secure processing without cloud dependency.

Batch Consistency
Make libraries searchable and uniform. Process folders or Photos Library in bulk for professional organization.

Extensible & Private
Built for power users. Extend sections, control every field, and keep full ownership of your data.

How It Works

1. Select Images
Choose folders, Photos Library, or drag-and-drop files. Supports all major formats.

2. Configure Models & Schema
Load vision models and define fields/prompts. Customize for your needs.

3. Generate Metadata
Process locally. Review and edit results before output.

4. Export & Apply
Output to sidecars, embeds, JSON/TXT, Photos, or Finder tags.

FAQ

Custom models?
